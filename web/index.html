A measurement workflow

Every network starts with decisions.

The reports contain observations. Annotation identifies mentions and actors, then associates offices and actor-sector codes with report evidence. The network connects actors who occur in the same textual unit.

    What a connection means

    W = BᵀB   ·   diagonal = 0

    Each actor is present at most once in a sentence, paragraph, or report. The edge weight counts distinct shared units. Repeated mentions inside one unit do not add weight.

    These are undirected co-occurrence edges. They do not establish interactions, alliances, or causal relationships.
