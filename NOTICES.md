## Third-party sources

United Nations Security Council reports are identified by document symbol, date and official URL in data/source_manifest.csv and the annotation bundle. Names, office titles and affiliation labels are retained as factual annotation fields with source identifiers. They are not biographies or independent verification of the underlying claim. No report paragraph, complete sentence, annex contribution, PDF, UN logo or report image is included.

Attributed annex material in S/2004/428 and S/2017/282 still lacks a documented reuse determination. The unchanged source archive stays private. Publishing this separately reviewed factual/analytical export does not authorize publication of those source bytes, and project licenses do not relicense UN or other third-party material.

CAMEO actor-sector identifiers are attributed to the [CAMEO manual](https://github.com/openeventdata/Dictionaries/blob/master/CAMEO.Manual.1.1b3.tex). The public site provides newly written short glossary explanations; it does not distribute the manual or copy its prose. CAMEO codes, role/specialty assignments, analytical unions and primary-support assignments are unchanged from the study. The glossary explains the interface and does not replace the full historical coding protocol.

## Evidence and reproducibility

L is the frozen model branch; A incorporates saved human decisions. R is a dependent same-researcher reference limited to 120 paragraphs in six reports. It is not independent corpus-wide ground truth. Grounding and completion checks do not establish semantic accuracy, causal assistance benefits, productivity or mental-burden effects.

Source observations, machine predictions, historical human decisions and analytical assumptions are distinct. Report-year observations are not tenure or continuous presence. Edges are undirected co-occurrence, not interaction, alliance or causation. Published advanced metrics are displayed only within the supported complete baseline scope.

Exact source reconstruction means reconstruction from archived extraction text. The historical PDF extractor is unknown; downloading official PDFs does not guarantee the same text bytes. The public site distributes paragraph hashes and Unicode codepoint coordinates, not those texts. New compatible local datasets do not inherit the study's review or verification status.

## Browser and hosting

This application has no analytics, external fonts, model calls or credential collection. Files selected in the explorer stay in browser memory and are not uploaded. GitHub Pages operates the hosting infrastructure and [records visitor IP addresses for security](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection). This application does not promise that hosting providers keep no logs.

The public explorer is separate from the unpublished manuscript and private research project. Refer to publication.json and docs/publication-review.md for the reviewed distribution scope and approval status.
